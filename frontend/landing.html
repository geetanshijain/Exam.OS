Institutional Architecture

Engineered for Verifiable Examination Integrity

ExamOS replaces fragmented legacy processes with a unified zero-trust workflow, guaranteeing that question assets remain locked until authorized extraction.

1. Structured Authoring & Dual Custody

Faculty setters submit structured question sets or syllabus parameters. Access is revoked immediately upon submission, requiring dual approval from Department Heads and Controllers.

2. Server-Enforced Time Locks

Decryption is locked to server-verified UTC clocks. Even administrative credentials cannot extract decrypted assets before the designated exam release window.

3. Forensic Attribution & Honeytokens

Legitimate extractions are dynamically watermarked across every page with the operator's IP and timestamp. Unauthorized queries trigger silent decoy payloads and log security alerts.

Security Reality Notice: Physical Camera & Screenshot Limitations Browser sandbox restrictions cannot reliably prevent external photography or physical camera capture. ExamOS addresses this limitation through server-side release scheduling, expiring session tokens, and forensic watermarking containing candidate roll numbers and timestamps.
Institutional Governance: Visual and interface controls enforce client workflows backed by server-side verification.
